Root Cause Navigator

An interactive walk-through for construction root cause investigations: start with a potential cause, drill down through design, materials, workmanship, plant, workforce, project management, or site conditions, and land on practical, field-tested guidance for each finding.

Root Cause Navigator — a field guide to finding what actually went wrong.

How to read the map

BranchWhere the cause originated — Design, Materials, Workmanship, Plant, Workforce, Project Management, or Site Conditions.
CategoryA named family of issues within that branch, e.g. "Design Coordination Issue" or "Approval/Hold-Point Issue".
CauseThe specific, actionable finding — this is where the guidance lives.
Root CauseThe systemic layer beneath every cause — was it a missing/unclear standard, or a hold point knowingly traded away against the programme?

Every specific cause you land on leads to the same checkpoint at the bottom of the map, where you can trace it one level deeper into either a Standards, Policies & Procedures gap or a Schedule & Production Pressure override — the two most common systemic reasons a cause actually happened.

One option per line. Customize these to match your own QA vocabulary — leave a list empty to fall back to the built-in defaults.

Attachments

Controls how photos and PDFs attached to NCRs are stored in this browser.

AI-Assisted Suggestions (optional)

Paste your deployed Worker URL to enable AI-suggested evidence/root-cause text alongside the built-in suggestions. Leave blank to use built-in suggestions only — the app works fully offline either way.

Data Management

Your project's investigations, NCRs, and settings live in Supabase, shared across everyone signed in to this project. Export a backup for an offline copy; restoring pushes the backup's contents back into Supabase.

Have investigations/NCRs from before Supabase was wired in? They may still be sitting in this browser's storage, unused since the switch.
