How to read the map

Problem CategoryThe broadest split — equipment, people, or external factors.
Major Intermediate Cause CategoryNarrows down which part of that branch is involved.
Intermediate Cause CategoryA named family of issues, e.g. "Design Issue" or "Procedure Issue".
Intermediate CauseThe specific, actionable cause — this is where the guidance lives.

Equipment-type branches (Process Equipment, Software, Material/Product, Utility Systems, Other Equipment) all share the same five cause categories: Design, Reliability Program, Documentation & Records, Material/Parts & Product, and Hazard/Defect Analysis. Personnel-type branches (Company, Contract, Third-party) all share the same six: Procedure, Human Factors, Training/Qualification, Supervision, Communication, and Personal Performance.
